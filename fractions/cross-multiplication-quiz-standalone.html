Adding Fractions with Different Denominators

a/b + c/d = (ad+bc)/bd — the cross-multiplication method. A 10-question check, easy to advanced. Each answer is graded the moment you click it.

Definitions

A quick recap before the quiz below.

Cross-multiplication

When two fractions have different denominators and neither divides evenly into the other, cross-multiply: multiply diagonally for the new numerator, and multiply the two denominators together for the new denominator. ab + cd = ad+bcbd

Example: 1/2 + 1/3 = (1×3 + 1×2) / (2×3) = 5/6

#1 mistake

Adding straight across — numerator + numerator, denominator + denominator. That is never allowed. 1/2 + 1/3 is NOT 2/5.
