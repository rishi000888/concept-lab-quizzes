Exponents & Indices — Concept Test

A closed-book concept test in four parts — Formula (recall the laws themselves), Core (integer indices), Extended (fractional & negative-fractional indices) and Exam-Style (IGCSE past-paper-style questions). No formula sheet is shown — each part is scored separately, with instant feedback as you answer.

F Formula Test · 10 questions Recall the laws of indices themselves — no reference sheet.
C Core Level · 13 questions Multiplication & division laws, power of a power, zero & negative indices, powers of products/quotients.
E Extended Level · 12 questions Fractional indices & roots, negative fractional indices, solving for an unknown index, combined-law simplification.
X Exam-Style · 10 questions Original questions written in the style of IGCSE past papers — multi-step, combining several laws.
The stopwatch starts as soon as you press Start. Formula, Core, Extended and Exam-Style scores are tracked separately — you'll see all four update live as you answer.
